## Platform

web

## Users

Decision-makers at Italian businesses looking for someone to build a website, web application or custom software:
- small and medium businesses, local/regional (Emilia-Romagna) and across Italy (remote work, no geographic limit);
- manufacturing and B2B companies with technical products, catalogs and data sheets (the Carandina profile);
- professionals and practices (studi tecnici, liberi professionisti, small businesses).

Typical situation: they need a digital tool or a site redesign, are weighing an agency against a freelancer, and want to understand quickly whether OCSW is reliable and capable before getting in touch.

## Product Purpose

ocsw.it presents the work of OCSW — Oscar Costanzelli Software, a freelance developer, and brings in contacts from businesses.
Success: a **direct contact**, meaning a phone call, an email or a form request. Any channel counts; the form is one channel among several, not the only goal.

## Positioning

- **One point of contact**: the client talks directly to the person who writes the code, from analysis through to support.
- **Software built ad hoc**: code written for the project, not prepackaged themes or templates.
- **Broad technical skills**: beyond websites, also web applications, management software, 3D viewers, catalogs and integrations.
- **Ongoing support**: maintenance and assistance after delivery.

## Operating Context

Method in four phases: Analisi, Progetto, Sviluppo, Supporto.
Services: Siti web · Applicazioni web · Software su misura.
The Laboratorio holds demos and personal projects with sample data, which show capabilities without client data.

## Product Principles

1. Concrete proof beats claims: show real work (Carandina, working demos), never statements you can't verify.
2. Make the direct relationship visible: one person, one point of contact, along the whole path.
3. Contact is always one step away: phone, email and form are easy to reach from every page.
4. Honesty about content: demos labeled as demos, missing data marked as placeholders.
